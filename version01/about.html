About The Home Workshop

The Home Workshop was created by Jacqui Hudson-Underwood to empower homeowners, apartment therapist, house flippers, and anyone that is interested in do-it-yourself home improvement. The hope is that this will be a place to build community with others gaining the courage to take control of their projects but need a little help getting there.
